Core Summary: This application does not collect, store, share, or request any type of personal or technical information from its users.

1. Data Collection and Types of Data

The Application has been developed under the principle of "Privacy by Design". No registration, account creation, or login is required to enjoy its features. Consequently:

  • Personal Data: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, unique device identifiers (such as IMEI or Android ID), or precise location metrics.
  • Technical Data: We do not extract usage statistics, performance logs, crash analysis (crash logs) tied to a user identity, or hardware configuration metrics.

2. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures

Since the Application does not collect any data, we guaranteed that:

  • No user information is transmitted to or stored on external servers or centralized cloud databases under the developer's control.
  • We do not share, sell, lease, rent, or trade any form of user metrics or personal details with third-party companies, ad networks, business partners, or analytics providers.

3. Device Permissions

To function seamlessly, Cube3DR operates completely locally on your mobile device. The Application does not request or require access to sensitive operating system functions or data stored on the handset, including but not limited to:

  • Camera or Microphone.
  • Contacts, Address Book, or Accounts.
  • Internal device storage or external SD cards.
  • GPS or network-based location tracking.

4. In-App Advertising and Purchases

To provide an uninterrupted, clean, and secure gameplay environment:

  • The Application does not contain advertisements promotional banners, or third-party ad networkjcomponents.
  • The Application does not feature In-App Purchases (IAP) or payment gateways inside the game ecosystem.

5. Children's Privacy

By strictly refraining from collecting any personal or tracking information, the Application aligns entirely with international child privacy safeguards, including the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). It offers an entirely safe and compliant environment for users of all ages.
